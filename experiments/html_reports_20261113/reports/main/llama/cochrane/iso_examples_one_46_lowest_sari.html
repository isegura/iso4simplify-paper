Four studies focused on abdominal closure, two on caesarean section, two on vaginal repair and two on hip replacement. The use of blunt needles reduced the risk of glove perforations with a relative risk (RR) of 0.46 (95% confidence interval (CI) 0.38 to 0.54) compared to sharp needles.
