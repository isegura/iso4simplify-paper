In contrast, none of these complications occurred in the early group. The trial also found that patients who had surgery early had a shorter hospital stay (by 1.25 days) and operating time (by 14.80 minutes) compared to those who had surgery late. Based on this single high-bias risk trial, it appears that performing gallbladder removal surgery early after diagnosis may reduce morbidity during the waiting period, shorten hospital stays, and reduce operating times. However, more trials are needed to confirm these findings and determine if earlier surgery is better than delayed surgery even with shorter waiting periods.
