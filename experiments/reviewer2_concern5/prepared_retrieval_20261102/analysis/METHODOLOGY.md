# Reproducible planned comparison

The frozen active protocol is ../ANALYSIS_PLAN.md (2 November preparation); older parent-folder plans are historical. Primary family: 18 paired retrieval-minus-few-metric SARI contrasts. Secondary family: 18 paired retrieval-minus-mean-random contrasts. Mean random averages selections 42/44/46 within each row. The 335 dataset/source-ID groups preserve shared PLABA IDs. The existing Concern 2 finite-sample-corrected clustered cell-means core is reused with 54 observed cells (18 settings × retrieval, fixed metric, mean random). Tests use dataset group count minus one degrees of freedom. BH is separate per 18-test family. Bootstrap jointly resamples document groups within dataset, 10,000 draws, seed 20261023. Intervals are pointwise, not multiplicity-adjusted. No post-hoc overall contrast or inference on other metrics is added.

All retrieval certificates, evaluation item hashes, input hashes, row alignments and reference arrays are checked. Historical fixed CSVs match the original statistics audit; random evaluations match their saved input/item hashes. Corpus BLEU is computed from fixed saved predictions with SacreBLEU defaults, not averaged item BLEU; random BLEU is a descriptive mean of three corpus scores. No model inference, semantic reevaluation, prediction editing or output exclusion is performed. Complete input/output hashes and bootstrap sufficient statistics/draws are retained in verification.json and bootstrap_draws.npz.
