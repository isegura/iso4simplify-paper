The remitting seronegative symmetrical synovitis with pitting edema (RS3PE) syndrome is a rare disease with swollen joints and pitting edema. RS3PE has been reported with a number of conditions that include polymyalgia rheumatica (a disorder that causes muscle pain and stiffness, mainly in the shoulders and hips), rheumatoid arthritis (arthritis whereby immune cells attack healthy cells), an immune system disorder that causes dry eyes and mouth called Sjögren's syndrome, and a type of arthritis linked to psoriasis (a skin disease causing red, itchy scaly patches).
